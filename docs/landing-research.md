Reviewed on 2026-10-30 after the user's request to broaden the research beyond
Hallmark. Source documents were read directly from GitHub. These are design
references, not new application dependencies; no third-party setup script ran.

| Project | What was reviewed | Useful here |

Hallmark remains the installed workflow. Other projects were researched for
principles; their installation and complete command workflows are not claimed
to have run. They do not justify replacing React/Vite/CSS or changing the studio.

## The user's product references

Public references: Periodica, Qarta, Once Upon, Popsa, Mixbook and Chatbooks.
Popsa's captured hero puts physical books beside a short promise and action.
Periodica introduces photo books before the catalogue; some remote hero material
did not finish loading in the inspection environment. Qarta could not be verified
because its HTTPS connection failed certificate validation; validation stayed on.
Once Upon's incorrect `/en` route returned 404; its root homepage was confirmed
separately, as were Mixbook and Chatbooks. No competitor images, prices, review
counts or guarantees were copied into Matiane.

V2 uses a product-led first screen, a consistent left edge and grid, one reading
direction and price/options before the gift invitation. The Russian proposition
is the user's exact wording. Sample layouts are not proof of delivered orders.
The restored design is intentionally not subjected to a new visual style: the
user explicitly asked to recover it.
