# verso identity concepts

These five original concept constructions were created for the verso review
page on 2026-11-04. They are proposals, not a selected global identity.

The [Logo Designer skill](https://github.com/neonwatty/logo-designer-skill/blob/main/skills/logo-designer/SKILL.md)
(MIT) provided the exploration and SVG validation workflow. No third-party logo
artwork, stock icons, generated raster imagery or customer files are included.

Each concept folder includes self-contained transparent `logo.svg`, `mark.svg`
and `wordmark.svg`. Logo and mark also have `-dark` (white foreground) and
`-mono` (black foreground) versions. All use paths, including the letters;
no external font resources are needed to open a downloaded SVG.

Font source and construction notes are in each folder's `notes.json`:

- Turn, Frame: Manrope Bold, `public/fonts/Matiane-manrope-Bold.ttf`.
- Index, Imprint: Noto Sans Condensed Bold, `public/fonts/NotoSans-CondensedBold.ttf`.
- Tact: Lora Regular, `public/fonts/Matiane-lora-Regular.ttf`.

These are existing licensed project fonts. Their source notices remain in
`public/fonts/manrope-OFL.txt`, `public/fonts/lora-OFL.txt` and `public/fonts/OFL.txt`.
Internal font filenames are retained for compatibility. The identity page uses
Noto Sans Georgian, its condensed bold cut, and Noto Serif Georgian for the
corresponding Georgian examples; the Latin brand name remains verso.
