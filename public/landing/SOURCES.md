# Landing assets

The lifestyle photographs are from Unsplash and are used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits commercial use.
Local WebP copies are resized and compressed for the public homepage. They are
demonstration photographs, never photographs from Matiane customer accounts.

Book mockups, page arrangements, illustrations and copy are created for Matiane.

The WOFF2 files are web subsets of fonts already distributed in `public/fonts`:

- `keepsake.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond Regular.
- `keepsake-italic.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond Regular Italic.
- `keepsake-georgian.woff2`: BPG Serif Modern.
- `humanist.woff2`: FiraGO Regular.

Original font authorship and licenses are retained in the subset name tables.
See the original Cormorant/OFL, BPG and FiraGO license files in `public/fonts`.

`spread-journal-{ka,en,ru}-{0,1,2}.webp`, `cover-journal-{ka,en,ru}.webp` and
`studio-journal-{ka,en,ru}.webp` are captures of the actual Matiane editor and
preview with these photographs and synthetic demonstration books. No customer
account, book, personal photograph or uploaded customer file is used. The
configuration image shows the front cover from that sample; its binding and
thickness are schematic visualisations, not printing-spine measurements.

`journal-display.woff2` is a web subset of Cormorant Garamond Bold, distributed
under `public/fonts/cormorantgaramond-OFL.txt`. All other source fonts and their
licenses are unchanged. The original font naming and license metadata remains
in the subset.

`journal-humanist.woff2` is the FiraGO Regular web subset, with Georgian, Latin,
Cyrillic and the Georgian lari sign (U+20BE), under `public/fonts/FiraGO-OFL.txt`.
