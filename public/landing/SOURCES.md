Book mockups, page arrangements, illustrations and copy are created for Matiane.

The WOFF2 files are web subsets of fonts already distributed in `public/fonts`:

- `keepsake.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond Regular.
- `keepsake-italic.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond Regular Italic.
- `keepsake-georgian.woff2`: BPG Serif Modern.
- `humanist.woff2`: FiraGO Regular.

Original font authorship and licenses are retained in the subset name tables.
See the original Cormorant/OFL, BPG and FiraGO license files in `public/fonts`.
