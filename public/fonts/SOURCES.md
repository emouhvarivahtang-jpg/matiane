# Matiane fonts

All bundled fonts are unmodified originals, free of charge and licensed for
commercial use, including embedding in PDF photo books. Font names and
copyright notices are retained. The font licenses do not license the customer's
photographs or the application itself.

GNU FreeFont corresponding editable sources are supplied in
[freefont-src-20120503.tar.gz](freefont-src-20120503.tar.gz), copied from
https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/freefont/freefont-src-20120503.tar.gz. Build instructions
and font author credits are included in that archive. The embedding exception
permits documents using the font or unaltered portions of it to keep their own
license, including commercial printed books and PDF exports.

Every offered family has all 33 modern Georgian Mkhedruli letters, basic Latin,
and Russian Cyrillic, in regular and bold. The additional Mtavruli uppercase
letters fall back to Noto Georgian where absent from older Unicode fonts, in
both the browser and PDF. Italic is synthesized with a matching slant.

The font picker contains nine distinct families and Noto Sans Condensed as a
tenth option. Legacy IDs `serif`, `sans` and `compact` remain supported.
